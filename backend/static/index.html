    Frequently asked questions

    Is ClipForge AI free?

    Yes. It runs on your own machine — no sign-up, no watermark, no limits beyond your computer's speed.

    Which links work?

    Public YouTube, Vimeo and most video sites supported by yt-dlp, plus direct MP4 links. Private, members-only or DRM-protected videos can't be imported.

    How long does it take?

    Usually 2–5 minutes: the download, then fast speech recognition on your Mac's GPU (a 20-minute video transcribes in about 2 minutes), then clips render two at a time.

    How many clips will I get?

    Choose 3, 5 or 8. Clips never overlap and always end on a complete sentence.

    Can I use any video?

    Only process videos you own or have permission to use.

    Connect your YouTube channel — one-time setup

    YouTube requires your own free Google API key so uploads go to your channel. Takes about 15 minutes, once.

    1. Open console.cloud.google.com → create a project named ClipForge.
    2. Go to APIs & Services → Library, search YouTube Data API v3 → Enable.
    3. OAuth consent screen (Google Auth Platform): User type External, app name ClipForge, your email. Under Audience → Test users add the Google account that owns your channel.
    4. Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID → type Web application.
      Authorised redirect URI: http://localhost:8000/api/youtube/callback
    5. Download JSON, rename it to client_secret.json and put it in clipforge-ai/backend/.
    6. Restart the server, then click Connect YouTube and sign in with your channel's account.

    Note: until Google verifies your app, YouTube keeps API uploads private — make them public in YouTube Studio.
